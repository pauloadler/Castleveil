# Playable Prototype

## Target loop
Enter the prototype dungeon, move and aim, fight normal enemies, gain XP, reach the boss, defeat or die to the boss, and restart after death.

## Controls
- WASD: movement
- Mouse: facing/aim
- LMB: basic attack
- RMB: heavy attack
- Space: dodge
- Q: Whirlwind
- E: Projectile

## Prototype content
- 1 player archetype
- 2 active skills
- 1 melee enemy
- 1 ranged enemy
- 1 elite
- 1 boss
- placeholder dungeon
- HP/Stamina/XP/Level HUD

## Setup expectation
The project should eventually expose `Tools > ARPG > Build Prototype` to create or refresh builder-owned prototype content.
